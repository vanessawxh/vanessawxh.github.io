Automated Matcha Maker

Year2025
OrganizationME 110, Product Development
RoleGroup Project
ToolsOnShape · Ansys Mechanical · Altair · Prusa
TagsEngineering · Design · CAD · Simulations · Electrical

Overview

Developed in a product design course, this project focused on streamlining the process of making matcha through an attachment designed for reusable bottles. The device integrates a spring-loaded dispensing mechanism for powdered matcha and a DC motor-driven whisking system, enabling near-autonomous preparation. Our team went through several design iterations informed by user feedback, balancing functionality with convenience. Using Onshape, we modeled the system with appropriate tolerancing for manufacturability and produced a working prototype via 3D printing. To validate performance, we conducted FEA and CFD simulations, ensuring smooth powder release and reliable operation.
