Overview

Continuity is established through a unified roof mass and circulation strategy driven by voids aligned to the roof's directional grid. These voids guide movement toward a central courtyard while anchoring vertical access at the street and greenway edges. Narrow glazing and clerestory windows highlight linear flow and enhance light along public-facing zones. The relationship between solid and void strengthens the spatial hierarchy, drawing focus toward the center of the site. This organization creates a gradual transition from public to private, reinforcing the courtyard as a semi-public core.
