Powertrain

Contributed to the redesign of a gas-powered Honda motorcycle by developing a custom motor mount for its electric drivetrain. Participated in CAD design and selection of mounting points on the frame. Helped choose 6061 aluminum with lightning holes for weight reduction and added a central steel beam for rigidity. Led the manufacturing process by operating a FabLight laser cutter to fabricate precision aluminum components. Gained hands-on experience in mechanical design, material selection, and fabrication within a collaborative engineering team.

Low Voltage

I worked on the Low Voltage team developing the bike dashboard. After comparing controllers for vibration tolerance, we selected an ESP32 instead of a Raspberry Pi due to the Pi's higher sensitivity to vibration. We then paired the ESP32 with a display using a protocol converter to resolve compatibility differences; the screen shows basic ride data such as speed and battery status.
