Overview

I took a Finite Element Method course at Berkeley that emphasized the hand-calculation foundations behind numerical simulation, giving me a solid intuition for how continuous boundary-value problems are converted into computable systems. The class walked through deriving the strong and weak forms of governing equations, discretizing domains into elements and nodes, and applying Dirichlet and Neumann boundary conditions, while introducing practical tools like Gaussian quadrature for accurate numerical integration. We studied 1D and 2D formulations, element types and mesh geometry choices, and how elementization affects accuracy and convergence; coursework also covered approximating stream flows, converting spatial problems to systems of ODEs, and marching solutions forward with time-stepping schemes. I implemented these concepts in MATLAB, building solvers for both 2D and 3D problems.
