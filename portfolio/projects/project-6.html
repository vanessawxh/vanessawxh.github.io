Roll Cage Optimization

Year2025
OrganizationFormula Sun Grand Prix, Altair Challenge
RolePartner Project
ToolsSiemens Altair
TagsEngineering · CAD · Simulations

Overview

Participated in a 3D CAD competition focused on structural optimization and design for manufacturability. Tasked with designing a roll cage for a pre-defined occupant cell, I applied topology optimization techniques to reduce weight while maintaining structural integrity. To ensure material consistency across all submissions, Steel 304 was mandated, requiring that all optimization efforts be achieved purely through geometric refinement. My design balanced manufacturability with performance by incorporating FEA-driven iteration, minimizing mass without compromising safety or stiffness.
