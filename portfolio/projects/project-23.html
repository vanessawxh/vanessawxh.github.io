GoBearTransit: A Transit App for Students

Year2024
OrganizationDES INV 15, Design Methodology
RoleGroup Project
ToolsFigma
TagsDesign · UIUX

Overview

Our team designed a transit app tailored to UC Berkeley students to address common campus transit problems: delays, inaccurate ETAs, overcrowding, and poor real-time updates, by combining reliable live tracking with intuitive planning and community features. The app provides live bus tracking, continually updated ETAs and delay notifications, and route optimization powered by existing bus and pedestrian data to generate campus-specific ETA paths. Beyond navigation, we prioritized clarity and usability while integrating social functionality: group trip planning, location sharing, and social leaderboards, to help students coordinate travel and build community. The core features are technically feasible (real-time tracking, route optimization, and trip planning), and future improvements include deeper campus integrations (for example, Dwinelle navigator) and more fully realized social tools to enhance the community experience.
