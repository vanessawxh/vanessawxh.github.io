Overview

Designed a carbon fiber composite occupant cell utilizing varied core materials to optimize stiffness-to-weight ratio and crash safety. Led the development process of CAD models to ensure structural integration with surrounding subsystems, including load-bearing interfaces for the array tilting mechanism. Created simulation models in ANSYS Mechanical and ANSYS ACP, defining boundary conditions, load cases, and laminate layups to evaluate structural integrity under expected service loads.
